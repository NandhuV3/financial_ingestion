# 000-project-charter.md

# Project Name

Partner Investing

---

# Mission

Build a production-grade investment intelligence platform that transforms company filings into durable, auditable, replayable investor intelligence.

The platform is designed for long-term investment understanding.

Not short-term trading.

Not a stock screener.

Not a chatbot.

Not a RAG demo.

Not a dashboard project.

LOCKED.

---

# Product Vision

Transform raw company disclosures into structured investment intelligence.

Every conclusion must be:

- Traceable
- Explainable
- Auditable
- Versioned
- Reproducible

The platform should answer:

Q1 — What does this company actually do?

Q2 — Where does the next rupee come from?

Q3 — Can management be trusted?

Q4 — Is the story already priced in?

Q5 — Why would an owner hold this business and what would change that?

LOCKED.

---

# Architectural Philosophy

The system is designed around:

Determinism first.

Governance over convenience.

Auditability over speed.

Replayability over flexibility.

LOCKED.
