# Investor Intelligence Artifact Specification

Status: LOCKED

## 1. Purpose

Investor Intelligence answers the Ownership Questions.

It is the terminal intelligence artifact.

No other layer answers Q1-Q5.

---

## 2. Position In Architecture

```text
Company Knowledge
      +
Quarter Understanding
      +
Business Signals
      +
Trust Signals
      +
Topic Evolution
      +
Market Data
      ↓
Investor Intelligence
```

---

## 3. Artifact Identity

```json
{
  "artifact_type": "investor_intelligence"
}
```

---

## 4. Artifact Structure

```json
{
  "company_id": "string",
  "period_id": "string",

  "questions": {
    "q1": {},
    "q2": {},
    "q3": {},
    "q4": {},
    "q5": {}
  }
}
```

---

```json
{
  "status":
    "answered |

  "confidence": 0.0,

  "evidence_package": {
    "company_knowledge_refs": [],

  }

```

---

## 6. Q1 Ownership

Question:

```text
What does this company actually sell?
```

Primary Source:

```text
Company Knowledge
```

Purpose:

Explain:

* products
* services
* customers
* value creation
* revenue model

Q1 owns canonical business explanation.

---

## 7. Q2 Ownership

Question:

```text
Where does the next rupee come from?
```

Primary Sources:

* Company Knowledge
* Quarter Understanding

Supporting Sources:

* Business Signals
* Topic Evolution

Purpose:

Explain:

* growth engines
* future revenue drivers
* business expansion mechanisms

Q2 is not a forecast.

---

## 8. Q3 Ownership

Question:

```text
Can the story be trusted?
```

Primary Sources:

* Quarter Understanding
* Trust Signals

Supporting Sources:

* Commitment Tracking

Purpose:

Produce trust conclusion.

Possible outcomes:

```text
Trust Strengthening
Trust Stable
Trust Weakening
Insufficient Evidence
```

Q3 owns the trust verdict.

---

## 9. Q4 Ownership

Question:

```text
Is the story already too expensive?
```

Primary Source:

```text
Market Data
```

Without Market Data:

```json
{
  "status": "insufficient_inputs"
}
```

Q4 must never invent valuation conclusions.

---

## 10. Q5 Ownership

Question:

```text
Why would I hold it and what would change that?
```

Primary Sources:

* Company Knowledge
* Quarter Understanding

Supporting Sources:

* Business Signals
* Trust Signals
* Topic Evolution

Purpose:

Produce:

* ownership thesis
* ownership rationale
* change conditions

Only Q5 owns change-condition generation.

---

## 11. Q5 Structure

```json
{
  "answer": "string",

  "hold_reasons": [
    "string"
  ],

  "change_conditions": [
    {
      "condition": "string",
      "severity":
        "low |
         medium |
         high"
    }
  ]
}
```

---

## 12. Allowed Reasoning

Investor Intelligence may:

* Produce ownership conclusions
* Produce trust conclusions
* Produce business-quality conclusions
* Produce ownership rationale
* Produce change conditions
* Produce investor-facing synthesis

This is the first layer allowed to think like an owner.

---

## 13. Forbidden Reasoning

Investor Intelligence must not:

* Create signals
* Create trust observations
* Create Company Knowledge
* Re-read filing content
* Bypass architecture

Investor Intelligence consumes intelligence.

It does not generate upstream intelligence.

---

## 14. Ownership Rule

Only Investor Intelligence answers:

```text
Q1
Q2
Q3
Q4
Q5
```

All upstream layers provide evidence.

Investor Intelligence provides conclusions.

---

## 15. Relationship To Partner Domain

Partner Domain may:

* format
* summarize
* visualize

Partner Domain may not:

* change answers
* create answers
* generate new ownership reasoning

Investor Intelligence remains the source of truth.
