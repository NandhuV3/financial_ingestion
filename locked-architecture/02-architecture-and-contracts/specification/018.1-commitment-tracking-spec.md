# Commitment Tracking Specification

Status: LOCKED

## 1. Purpose

Commitment Tracking exists to extract, store, and track the lifecycle of management commitments across filing periods.

Commitment Tracking answers:

> What commitments did management make, and what happened to them?

Commitment Tracking is a trust-evidence layer.

It does not determine whether management is trustworthy.

It does not produce trust conclusions.

It does not produce investor conclusions.

It tracks commitment behavior.

---

## 2. Position In Architecture

```text
Filing Artifact
        +
Company Knowledge
        +
Prior Commitment Tracking
        ↓
Commitment Tracking
        ↓
Trust Signals
```

Commitment Tracking provides evidence.

Trust Signals interpret commitment evidence into trust observations.

---

## 3. Inputs

### Required Inputs

* Filing Artifact
* Prior Commitment Tracking Artifact

Commitment Tracking requires historical commitment state.

Without prior history, lifecycle tracking cannot occur.

---

### Enrichment Inputs

* Company Knowledge

Company Knowledge may provide context for identifying strategic commitments.

Company Knowledge must not be used to determine commitment outcomes.

---

## 4. Forbidden Inputs

* Themes
* Topic Evolution
* Quarter Change
* Business Signals
* Trust Signals
* Quarter Understanding
* Investor Intelligence
* Market Data

Commitment Tracking operates directly on management commitments.

It must not consume downstream interpretation.

---

## 5. Core Question

Commitment Tracking answers:

```text
What did management commit to?
What is the current status of that commitment?
```

Commitment Tracking does not answer:

```text
Was the commitment good?
Did investors benefit?
Can management be trusted?
```

Those belong downstream.

---

## 6. Commitment Definition

A commitment is a forward-looking management statement that can later be evaluated against observed behavior.

Examples:

* We will expand datacenter capacity.
* We expect to launch a product.
* We intend to improve operating margins.
* We plan to enter a new market.
* We expect supply constraints to ease.

A commitment must contain:

* future-oriented language
* identifiable objective
* observable outcome

---

## 7. Non-Commitments

The following are not commitments:

### Historical Facts

Example:

```text
Revenue increased 12%.
```

---

### Current-State Descriptions

Example:

```text
We operate globally.
```

---

### Generic Aspirations

Example:

```text
We seek long-term success.
```

---

### Boilerplate Statements

Example:

```text
We continue to focus on shareholder value.
```

Unless tied to a measurable future objective.

## 9. Supported Lifecycle States

### Open

Definition:

Commitment exists.

No resolution evidence yet.

---

### Fulfilled

Definition:

Evidence indicates the commitment was completed.

Example:

```text
Commitment:
Expand datacenter capacity.

Later filing:
New datacenter capacity deployed.
```

---

### Revised

Definition:

Management changes the commitment.

Examples:

* timeline changed
* scope changed
* objective changed

The commitment remains active.

---

### Abandoned

Definition:

Management no longer pursues the commitment.

Evidence indicates withdrawal or discontinuation.

---

### Overdue

Definition:

Expected completion has not occurred within the implied or stated timeframe.

No fulfillment evidence exists.

---

## 10. Allowed Reasoning

Commitment Tracking may:

* Extract commitments
* Compare commitments across periods
* Detect fulfillment evidence
* Detect revisions
* Detect abandonment
* Detect overdue status
* Track commitment duration
* Track commitment lifecycle state

Commitment Tracking may answer:

```text
What happened to the commitment?
```

---

## 11. Forbidden Reasoning

Commitment Tracking must not:

* Produce trust conclusions
* Produce investor conclusions
* Judge management quality
* Judge management credibility
* Judge commitment importance
* Explain business implications

Invalid:

```text
Management failed to deliver.
```

Reason:

Judgment.

---

Invalid:

```text
This weakens trust.
```

Reason:

Trust Signals owns trust observations.

---

Invalid:

```text
Investors should be concerned.
```

Reason:

Investor Intelligence owns investor conclusions.

---

Invalid:

```text
Management appears unreliable.
```

Reason:

Trust assessment is forbidden.

---

## 12. Ownership Boundaries

### Commitment Tracking vs Trust Signals

| Commitment Tracking | Trust Signals             |
| ------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Commitment evidence | Trust observations        |
| Lifecycle tracking  | Trust interpretation      |
| What happened?      | What trust signal exists? |

Example:

Commitment Tracking:

```text
AI capacity expansion commitment abandoned.
```

Trust Signals:

```text
Commitment Abandoned signal generated.
```

---

### Commitment Tracking vs Quarter Understanding

| Commitment Tracking | Quarter Understanding        |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Commitment status   | Meaning of commitment status |
| Evidence            | Interpretation               |

Example:

Commitment Tracking:

```text
Status = overdue
```

Quarter Understanding:

```text
Several strategic commitments remain unresolved.
```

---

### Commitment Tracking vs Investor Intelligence

| Commitment Tracking | Investor Intelligence |
| ------------------- | --------------------- |
| Commitment evidence | Trust conclusion      |
| Lifecycle state     | Ownership impact      |

Example:

Commitment Tracking:

```text
Three commitments were abandoned.
```

Investor Intelligence:

```text
Trust weakened because multiple commitments were abandoned.
```

---

## 13. Relationship To Ownership Questions

### Q1 Ownership

No contribution.

---

### Q2 Ownership

Indirect contribution only.

May provide context regarding execution of growth initiatives.

---

### Q3 Ownership

Primary evidence contribution.

Commitment Tracking provides longitudinal follow-through evidence.

Trust conclusions remain downstream.

---

### Q4 Ownership

No contribution.

---

### Q5 Ownership

Indirect contribution.

Provides evidence that may later affect ownership thesis stability.

Does not identify change conditions.

---

## 14. Execution Model

Execution Type:

```text
Deterministic + LLM Extraction
```

Extraction:

* LLM-assisted commitment identification

Lifecycle Tracking:

* Deterministic

Status Assignment:

* Deterministic

Commitment Tracking is an evidence layer.

It is not an interpretation layer.

It is not a trust-verdict layer.

It is not an investor-intelligence layer.

---

## 15. Golden Rule

Commitment Tracking does not determine whether management can be trusted.

Commitment Tracking determines only:

```text
What management said.
What management later did.
Current commitment status.
```

Trust conclusions belong downstream.
