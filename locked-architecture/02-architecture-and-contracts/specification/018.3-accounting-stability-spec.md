# Accounting Stability Specification

Status: LOCKED

## 1. Purpose

Accounting Stability exists to track how the company's accounting presentation evolves across filing periods.

Accounting Stability answers:

> Has the company changed the way it reports its business?

Accounting Stability is a trust-evidence layer.

It records accounting-related changes over time.

It does not determine whether those changes are good or bad.

It does not determine whether management is trustworthy.

It does not produce investor conclusions.

---

## 2. Position In Architecture

```text
Filing Artifact
        +
Prior Accounting Stability
        ↓
Accounting Stability
        ↓
Trust Signals
```

Accounting Stability provides evidence.

Trust Signals interpret accounting evidence into trust observations.

---

## 3. Inputs

### Required Inputs

* Filing Artifact
* Prior Accounting Stability Artifact

Historical accounting records are required to identify changes across periods.

---

### Enrichment Inputs

* Company Knowledge

Company Knowledge may provide stable business context when matching renamed business segments.

It must not determine Accounting Stability outputs.

---

## 4. Forbidden Inputs

* Quarter Change
* Business Signals
* Trust Signals
* Quarter Understanding
* Investor Intelligence
* Market Data

Accounting Stability operates only on accounting-related filing disclosures.

It must not consume downstream interpretation.

---

## 5. Core Question

Accounting Stability answers:

```text
How has the company's accounting presentation changed over time?
```

Accounting Stability does **not** answer:

```text
Are these accounting changes suspicious?

Can management be trusted?

Should investors worry?
```

Those belong downstream.

---

## 6. Responsibilities

Accounting Stability records accounting-related changes including:

* Accounting policy changes
* Segment reporting changes
* Non-GAAP reporting evolution
* Financial statement restatements
* Disclosure consistency

Accounting Stability records what changed.

It does not explain why.

---

## 7. Outputs

Accounting Stability produces accounting observation records.

Example:

```json
{
  "change_type": "ACCOUNTING_POLICY_CHANGED",

  "policy_name": "Revenue Recognition",

  "first_detected_period": "2026-Q2",

  "description": "Management disclosed a change in revenue recognition policy.",

  "confidence": 0.95
}
```

---

## 8. Accounting Policy Changes

Purpose:

Track formal accounting policy changes disclosed by management.

Examples:

* Revenue Recognition Policy
* Lease Accounting
* Inventory Valuation
* Depreciation Method
* Impairment Methodology

Accounting Stability records:

* policy changed
* policy introduced
* policy removed

Accounting Stability does not determine whether the policy is appropriate.

---

## 9. Segment Reporting Changes

Purpose:

Track changes in business segment reporting.

Examples:

* Segment renamed
* Segment merged
* Segment split
* New reportable segment
* Removed reportable segment

Accounting Stability records structural reporting changes.

It does not infer business motivation.

---

## 10. Non-GAAP Evolution

Purpose:

Track changes in Non-GAAP reporting across periods.

Examples:

* New Non-GAAP metric introduced
* Existing metric removed
* Calculation methodology changed
* Additional adjustment categories added
* Adjustment categories removed

Accounting Stability records reporting evolution.

It does not judge reporting quality.

---

## 11. Financial Restatements

Purpose:

Track formal restatements disclosed in filings.

Examples:

* Revenue restatement
* Earnings restatement
* Cash flow restatement
* Balance sheet restatement

Accounting Stability records:

* what was restated
* when it was restated
* filing references

It does not determine materiality.

---

## 12. Disclosure Consistency

Purpose:

Track consistency of accounting-related disclosures across periods.

Examples:

* Consistent policy disclosure
* Expanded disclosure
* Reduced disclosure
* Newly disclosed accounting estimate
* Removed disclosure

Accounting Stability records disclosure behavior.

It does not interpret disclosure quality.

---

## 13. Allowed Reasoning

Accounting Stability may:

* Detect accounting policy changes
* Detect segment reporting changes
* Detect Non-GAAP evolution
* Detect financial restatements
* Compare accounting disclosures across periods
* Match equivalent accounting concepts across filings
* Record structural accounting changes

Accounting Stability answers:

```text
What accounting-related changes occurred?
```

---

## 14. Forbidden Reasoning

Accounting Stability must not:

* Detect fraud
* Detect earnings manipulation
* Judge accounting quality
* Produce trust conclusions
* Produce investor conclusions
* Assess management credibility
* Explain why accounting changes occurred
* Determine materiality beyond explicit filing disclosures

Invalid:

```text
Management manipulated earnings.
```

Reason:

Fraud detection is outside this layer.

---

Invalid:

```text
The accounting changes reduce confidence.
```

Reason:

Trust interpretation belongs downstream.

---

Invalid:

```text
Investors should be concerned.
```

Reason:

Investor Intelligence owns investor conclusions.

---

Invalid:

```text
Management intentionally changed reporting.
```

Reason:

Intent attribution is forbidden.

---

## 15. Ownership Boundaries

### Accounting Stability vs Trust Signals

| Accounting Stability        | Trust Signals                  |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Accounting evidence         | Trust observations             |
| Records accounting behavior | Interprets accounting evidence |
| What changed?               | What trust signal exists?      |

Example:

Accounting Stability:

```text
Segment reporting methodology changed.
```

Trust Signals:

```text
Accounting Stability signal generated.
```

---

### Accounting Stability vs Quarter Change

| Accounting Stability | Quarter Change         |
| -------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Accounting reporting | Business understanding |
| Reporting changes    | Business changes       |

Accounting Stability focuses on reporting structure.

Quarter Change focuses on business structure.

---

### Accounting Stability vs Business Signals

| Accounting Stability | Business Signals      |
| -------------------- | --------------------- |
| Accounting evidence  | Business observations |

Accounting Stability never produces business signals.

---

---

## 18. Golden Rule

Accounting Stability does not determine whether management's accounting is trustworthy.

Accounting Stability determines only:

```text
What accounting policies changed.
What reporting structure changed.
What Non-GAAP reporting changed.
What financial restatements occurred.
How accounting disclosures evolved.
```

Trust conclusions belong downstream.
