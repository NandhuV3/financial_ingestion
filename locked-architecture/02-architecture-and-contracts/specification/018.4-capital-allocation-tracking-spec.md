# Capital Allocation Tracking Specification

Status: LOCKED

## 1. Purpose

Capital Allocation Tracking exists to compare what management states it intends to do with capital against what the filing reports was actually done.

Capital Allocation Tracking answers:

> Did observed capital deployment align with management's stated capital allocation priorities?

It is a Trust Architecture evidence layer.

It records alignment and gaps between stated priorities and observed deployment.

It does not determine whether management made good decisions.

It does not evaluate capital allocation quality.

It does not produce trust conclusions.

---

## 2. Position In Architecture

```text
Filing Artifact
        +
Financial Statements
        +
Company Knowledge
        +
Prior Capital Allocation Tracking
        ↓
Capital Allocation Tracking
        ↓
Trust Signals
```

Capital Allocation Tracking produces evidence.

Trust Signals interpret that evidence.

---

## 3. Inputs

### Required Inputs

* Filing Artifact
* Financial Statements
* Prior Capital Allocation Tracking Artifact

The Filing provides stated capital allocation priorities.

Financial Statements provide observed deployment.

Prior artifacts provide longitudinal tracking.

---

### Enrichment Inputs

* Company Knowledge

Company Knowledge provides durable business context when interpreting stated priorities.

It must not determine outputs.

---

## 4. Forbidden Inputs

* Quarter Change
* Business Signals
* Trust Signals
* Quarter Understanding
* Investor Intelligence
* Market Data

Capital Allocation Tracking operates before downstream interpretation.

---

## 5. Core Question

Capital Allocation Tracking answers:

```text
Did observed capital deployment align with management's stated priorities?
```

It does **not** answer:

```text
Was management right?

Was capital allocation effective?

Should investors trust management?

Was shareholder capital well allocated?
```

Those belong downstream.

---

## 6. Responsibilities

Capital Allocation Tracking performs four responsibilities.

### 6.1 Extract Stated Capital Priorities

Identify explicit statements describing intended capital deployment.

Examples:

* AI infrastructure investment
* Datacenter expansion
* Share repurchases
* Strategic acquisitions
* Research and development
* Security investment

Only explicitly stated priorities are extracted.

---

### 6.2 Record Observed Capital Deployment

Record deployment described in the filing.

Examples:

* Capital expenditures increased
* Share repurchases executed
* Acquisition completed
* Debt repayment
* Dividend payment
* Infrastructure expansion spending

Observed deployment comes from filing evidence.

---

### 6.3 Compare Stated Priorities Against Deployment

Determine whether observed deployment exists for each stated priority.

Possible observations:

* Supported
* Partially Supported
* Not Observed

These are evidence classifications.

They are not judgments.

---

### 6.4 Track Longitudinal Alignment

Track alignment over multiple periods.

Examples:

* Priority continues across periods.
* Deployment continues across periods.
* Gap persists across periods.
* Priority removed.
* New priority introduced.

Capital Allocation Tracking records behavior.

It does not explain behavior.

---

## 8. Alignment Status

Allowed values:

* SUPPORTED
* PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED
* NOT_OBSERVED

Definitions

### SUPPORTED

Observed filing evidence directly supports the stated priority.

Example:

Management:

> Expand AI datacenter capacity.

Observed:

Capital expenditures increased for datacenter expansion.

---

### PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED

Some deployment evidence exists but does not fully support the stated priority.

Example:

Management announces broad AI expansion.

Observed deployment is limited to one business area.

---

### NOT_OBSERVED

No filing-supported deployment evidence was identified.

This does **not** imply management failed.

It records only that deployment was not observed in this filing.

---

## 9. Allowed Reasoning

Capital Allocation Tracking may:

* Extract stated capital allocation priorities.
* Record observed deployment.
* Match priorities with deployment.
* Detect missing deployment evidence.
* Track alignment over time.
* Produce structured alignment records.

It answers:

```text
What did management say?

What deployment was observed?

How do those compare?
```

---

## 10. Forbidden Reasoning

Capital Allocation Tracking must not:

* Judge capital allocation quality.
* Produce trust conclusions.
* Assess management credibility.
* Infer management intent.
* Predict future deployment.
* Produce investor conclusions.
* Explain why deployment differed.
* Assess shareholder value creation.

Invalid:

```text
Management failed to execute.
```

Reason:

Execution assessment belongs downstream.

---

Invalid:

```text
Management allocated capital effectively.
```

Reason:

Quality judgments are forbidden.

---

Invalid:

```text
Management broke its promises.
```

Reason:

Trust verdicts belong to Trust Signals and Investor Intelligence.

---

Invalid:

```text
Investors should be concerned.
```

Reason:

Investor conclusions belong downstream.

---

Invalid:

```text
Management intentionally reduced investment.
```

Reason:

Intent attribution is forbidden.

---

## 11. Ownership Boundaries

### Capital Allocation Tracking vs Trust Signals

| Capital Allocation Tracking | Trust Signals                  |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Alignment evidence          | Trust observations             |
| Records deployment          | Interprets deployment behavior |
| No trust reasoning          | Trust dimension only           |

Example

Capital Allocation Tracking:

```text
AI infrastructure investment was stated.

Capital expenditures supporting AI infrastructure were observed.
```

Trust Signals:

```text
No Capital Allocation trust signal generated.
```

---

### Capital Allocation Tracking vs Quarter Change

| Capital Allocation Tracking  | Quarter Change  |
| ---------------------------- | --------------- |
| Capital deployment alignment | Business change |
| Trust evidence               | Business delta  |

Quarter Change explains business changes.

Capital Allocation Tracking records capital behavior.

---

### Capital Allocation Tracking vs Business Signals

| Capital Allocation Tracking | Business Signals  |
| --------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Trust evidence              | Business evidence |

No overlap.

## 14. Golden Rule

Capital Allocation Tracking does not determine whether management allocated capital correctly.

Capital Allocation Tracking determines only:

```text
What capital priorities management stated.

What capital deployment was reported.

Whether deployment supporting those priorities was observed.

How alignment changed across periods.
```

Trust conclusions belong to Trust Signals.

Investor conclusions belong to Investor Intelligence.
