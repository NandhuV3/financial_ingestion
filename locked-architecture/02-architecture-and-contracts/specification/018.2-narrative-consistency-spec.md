# Narrative Consistency Specification

Status: LOCKED

## 1. Purpose

Narrative Consistency exists to track how management's strategic narrative evolves across filing periods.

Narrative Consistency answers:

> Is management describing the business in a consistent way over time?

Narrative Consistency is a trust-evidence layer.

It does not determine whether management can be trusted.

It does not produce trust conclusions.

It does not produce investor conclusions.

It records narrative behavior.

---

## 2. Position In Architecture

```text
Filing Artifact
        +
Company Knowledge
        +
Prior Narrative Consistency
        ↓
Narrative Consistency
        ↓
Trust Signals
```

Narrative Consistency provides evidence.

Trust Signals interpret narrative evidence into trust observations.

---

## 3. Inputs

### Required Inputs

* Filing Artifact
* Prior Narrative Consistency Artifact

Narrative Consistency requires historical narrative records.

Without historical records, consistency cannot be evaluated.

---

### Enrichment Inputs

* Company Knowledge

Company Knowledge may help identify strategic priorities and business concepts.

Company Knowledge must not determine consistency outcomes.

---

## 4. Forbidden Inputs

* Quarter Change
* Business Signals
* Trust Signals
* Quarter Understanding
* Investor Intelligence
* Market Data

Narrative Consistency operates on management narrative history.

It must not consume downstream interpretation.

---

## 5. Core Question

Narrative Consistency answers:

```text
What did management say?
How has that narrative changed over time?
```

Narrative Consistency does not answer:

```text
Can management be trusted?
Should investors believe management?
```

Those belong downstream.

---

## 6. Strategic Priority Definition

A Strategic Priority is a business objective, initiative, or area of emphasis repeatedly communicated by management.

Examples:

* AI Infrastructure Expansion
* Cloud Capacity Growth
* Security Platform Investment
* Productivity Platform Expansion
* Margin Improvement
* International Expansion

Strategic Priorities are tracked longitudinally.

---

## 7. Outputs

Narrative Consistency produces narrative tracking records.

Example:

```json
{
  "priority_id": "priority_ai_infrastructure",

  "priority_name": "AI Infrastructure Expansion",

  "first_observed_period": "2025-Q1",

  "latest_observed_period": "2026-Q3",

  "status": "persistent",

  "language_shift_detected": false,

  "silent_drop_detected": false,

  "explanation_quality": "specific",

  "confidence": 0.91
}
```

---

## 8. Strategic Priority States

### Persistent

Definition:

Priority continues to appear across periods.

Example:

```text
AI Infrastructure Investment
mentioned every quarter.
```

---

### Emerging

Definition:

Priority appears after previously being absent.

Example:

```text
AI Agents
first appears in filings.
```

---

### Disappeared

Definition:

Previously recurring priority no longer appears.

Example:

```text
Metaverse Investment
no longer discussed.
```

---

### Reframed

Definition:

Priority remains present but is described differently.

Example:

Earlier:

```text
AI Research Investment
```

Later:

```text
AI Product Commercialization
```

Priority remains.

Narrative framing changes.

---

## 9. Language Shift Detection

Narrative Consistency tracks meaningful changes in management language.

Purpose:

Detect changes in how management communicates a priority.

Example:

Earlier:

```text
Experimental AI initiative
```

Later:

```text
Core commercial growth driver
```

Output:

```text
language_shift_detected = true
```

Narrative Consistency records the shift.

It does not explain the shift.

---

## 10. Silent Drop Detection

Narrative Consistency tracks when previously emphasized priorities disappear without explanation.

Example:

```text
AI Expansion
mentioned for six quarters.

No longer mentioned.
```

Output:

```text
silent_drop_detected = true
```

Narrative Consistency records the disappearance.

It does not determine whether the disappearance matters.

---

## 11. Explanation Quality

Narrative Consistency evaluates the specificity of management explanations.

Purpose:

Track whether management explanations become more detailed or more generic over time.

---

### Specific Explanation

Example:

```text
Datacenter expansion delayed because
GPU supply remained constrained.
```

Specific cause provided.

---

### Generic Explanation

Example:

```text
Market conditions affected results.
```

No concrete explanation provided.

---

### Explanation Quality Values

```text
specific
generic
mixed
insufficient_evidence
```

Narrative Consistency records explanation quality.

It does not interpret its significance.

---

## 12. Allowed Reasoning

Narrative Consistency may:

* Extract strategic priorities
* Track strategic priorities
* Compare priority language across periods
* Detect language shifts
* Detect narrative reframing
* Detect silent drops
* Track explanation quality
* Measure narrative persistence

Narrative Consistency may answer:

```text
How has management's narrative changed?
```

---

## 13. Forbidden Reasoning

Narrative Consistency must not:

* Produce trust conclusions
* Produce investor conclusions
* Judge management credibility
* Judge management honesty
* Judge management quality
* Explain why narrative changes occurred
* Explain investor implications

Invalid:

```text
Management appears less trustworthy.
```

Reason:

Trust assessment is forbidden.

---

Invalid:

```text
The language shift is concerning.
```

Reason:

Investor interpretation is forbidden.

---

Invalid:

```text
Investors should question management.
```

Reason:

Investor Intelligence owns investor conclusions.

---

Invalid:

```text
Management intentionally stopped discussing the initiative.
```

Reason:

Intent attribution is forbidden.

---

## 14. Ownership Boundaries

### Narrative Consistency vs Trust Signals

| Narrative Consistency | Trust Signals             |
| --------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Narrative evidence    | Trust observations        |
| Language tracking     | Trust interpretation      |
| What changed?         | What trust signal exists? |

Example:

Narrative Consistency:

```text
Priority silently disappeared.
```

Trust Signals:

```text
Strategic Priority Dropped signal generated.
```

---

### Narrative Consistency vs Topic Evolution

| Narrative Consistency | Topic Evolution        |
| --------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Management narrative  | Topic behavior         |
| Strategic priorities  | Canonical topics       |
| Trust evidence        | Business topic history |

Narrative Consistency focuses on management communication.

Topic Evolution focuses on Topic Registry concepts.

---

### Narrative Consistency vs Quarter Understanding

| Narrative Consistency | Quarter Understanding    |
| --------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Narrative evidence    | Narrative interpretation |
| What changed?         | Why it matters?          |

Narrative Consistency records.

Quarter Understanding interprets.

---

## 17. Golden Rule

Narrative Consistency does not determine whether management can be trusted.

Narrative Consistency determines only:

```text
What management said.
How management's narrative changed.
Whether priorities persisted.
Whether priorities disappeared.
How specific management explanations were.
```

Trust conclusions belong downstream.
