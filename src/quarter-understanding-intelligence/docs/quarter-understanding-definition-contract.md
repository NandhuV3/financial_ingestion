# Quarter Understanding Definition Contract

## 1. Definition

A Quarter Understanding is a synthesized interpretation of multiple business signals within the context of Company Knowledge.

It explains:

* What appears to be happening
* Why it may matter
* How multiple signals connect

It does not provide recommendations.

---

## 2. Signal vs Understanding

Business Signals identify observations.

Quarter Understanding explains those observations.

Example:

```text
Signal:
Cloud revenue acceleration observed.

Understanding:
Cloud remains a major growth engine and demand appears strong enough to justify continued infrastructure investment.
```

Signals are atomic.

Understandings are synthesized.

---

## 3. Understanding vs Fact

Company Knowledge:

```text
Microsoft provides cloud services.
```

Understanding:

```text
Cloud appears increasingly central to Microsoft's strategic priorities.
```

Facts describe the business.

Understandings interpret developments around the business.

---

## 4. Understanding vs Question

Understanding:

```text
AI infrastructure spending continues increasing.
```

Owner Question:

```text
Will future AI demand justify current spending levels?
```

Understandings explain.

Questions investigate.

---

## 5. Understanding vs Narrative

Understanding:

```text
Management appears focused on expanding AI capacity.
```

Narrative:

```text
The company is making one of its largest infrastructure bets in years.
```

Understandings remain concise and analytical.

Narratives are storytelling layers.

---

## 6. Understanding Characteristics

Every understanding should be:

* Evidence-based
* Traceable
* Auditable
* Period-specific
* Explainable
* Reproducible

Every understanding should be attributable to:

* Company Knowledge
* Business Signals
* Source filings

---

## 7. Understanding Categories

Future categories may include:

* Revenue
* Growth
* Margin
* Customer
* Product
* Competitive
* Operational
* Dependency
* Capital Allocation
* Management Commentary

These categories classify interpretation domains.

---

## 8. Understanding Lifecycle

```text
Company Knowledge
        +
Business Signals
        ↓
Quarter Understanding
        ↓
Owner Questions
        ↓
Narratives
```

Quarter Understanding is the first interpretation layer.

---

## 9. Required Properties

Every future understanding should conceptually support:

* Identity
* Category
* Summary
* Importance
* Confidence
* Evidence References
* Business Context
* Lineage

---

## 10. Non Goals

Quarter Understanding does not define:

* Recommendations
* Questions
* Narratives
* Portfolio actions
* Company facts

Quarter Understanding exists solely to interpret the quarter.
