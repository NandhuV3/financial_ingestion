# Quarter Understanding Ownership Contract

## 1. Purpose

Quarter Understanding owns interpretation.

It answers:

* What appears to be happening in the business this quarter?
* Why do the observed signals matter?
* Which business developments deserve attention?
* How should multiple signals be interpreted together?

Quarter Understanding converts observations into business understanding.

It does not generate recommendations, investment advice, owner questions, or narratives.

---

## 2. Position In Architecture

```text
Source Filings
    ↓
Structured Intelligence
    ↓
Company Knowledge
    ↓
Business Signals
    ↓
Quarter Understanding
    ↓
Owner Questions
    ↓
Narratives
```

Quarter Understanding sits between observation and owner-facing intelligence.

---

## 3. Quarter Understanding Owns

Quarter Understanding owns:

* Interpretation
* Contextual reasoning
* Signal synthesis
* Business implications
* Relative importance assessment
* Cross-signal understanding
* Quarter-specific business understanding

Examples:

```text
Business Signal:
Cloud demand acceleration observed.

Quarter Understanding:
Cloud demand remains a primary growth driver and management continues prioritizing infrastructure expansion.
```

---

## 4. Quarter Understanding Does Not Own

Quarter Understanding does not own:

* Company facts
* Business Signals
* Recommendations
* Buy/Sell opinions
* Portfolio guidance
* Position sizing
* Owner questions
* Narratives
* Dashboard presentation

---

## 5. Relationship To Company Knowledge

Company Knowledge owns durable business facts.

Quarter Understanding consumes Company Knowledge as context.

Quarter Understanding may reference Company Knowledge.

Quarter Understanding may not modify Company Knowledge.

Example:

Company Knowledge:

```text
Microsoft generates revenue from cloud services.
```

Quarter Understanding:

```text
Cloud remains the dominant strategic focus this quarter.
```

---

## 6. Relationship To Business Signals

Business Signals identify observations.

Quarter Understanding interprets observations.

Business Signals:

```text
Cloud revenue acceleration observed.
AI infrastructure investment increased.
Customer demand remained strong.
```

Quarter Understanding:

```text
The company appears willing to sacrifice near-term efficiency to expand cloud and AI capacity in support of sustained demand.
```

---

## 7. Relationship To Owner Questions

Quarter Understanding is an input.

Owner Questions are downstream outputs.

Quarter Understanding:

```text
Cloud investment intensity continues increasing.
```

Owner Question:

```text
Can future cloud profitability offset today's infrastructure spending?
```

Quarter Understanding should not generate that question.

---

## 8. Relationship To Narratives

Quarter Understanding produces structured interpretations.

Narratives produce owner-facing storytelling.

Quarter Understanding:

```text
AI investment appears strategically important.
```

Narrative:

```text
Management is making a significant bet on AI infrastructure despite near-term cost pressure.
```

Narrative ownership remains separate.

---

## 9. Ownership Principles

### Interpretation Ownership

Quarter Understanding owns interpretation.

### No Fact Ownership

Company Knowledge remains the source of truth for facts.

### No Observation Ownership

Business Signals remain the source of truth for observations.

### No Recommendation Ownership

Quarter Understanding must never recommend actions.

### No Question Ownership

Quarter Understanding must never generate owner questions.

### No Narrative Ownership

Quarter Understanding must not become a storytelling layer.

---

## 10. Non Goals

This phase does not define:

* Schemas
* Builders
* Repositories
* Storage
* Prompts
* Runtime logic
* Generated artifacts

This document defines ownership boundaries only.
